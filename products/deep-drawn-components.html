Deep drawn components

Precision deep-drawn housings, pans, domes and covers manufactured to close tolerances for automotive and industrial applications. Our deep-drawing capability handles a range of materials including mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

Capabilities

  • Progressive and transfer die tooling
  • In-house tool design and development
  • Materials: mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium
  • Surface treatment and finishing

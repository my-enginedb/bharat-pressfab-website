About the company

Engineering excellence in sheet metal manufacturing since 1991

Bharat Pressfab Private Limited (formerly Bharat Automotive Pressings (India) Private Limited) is a Pune-based engineering and manufacturing company with over 35 years of experience in sheet-metal components, precision pressings and fabricated assemblies.

Established in 1991, we serve OEMs and Tier-1 customers across the automotive, agricultural equipment, earthmoving, railway and industrial equipment sectors, along with other engineering applications.

Our integrated manufacturing capabilities include mechanical and hydraulic presses ranging from 50 to 500 tonnes, laser cutting, bending, pipe bending, welding, surface treatment, and in-house tool and die development.

Supported by experienced engineering teams, structured manufacturing systems and professional departmental leadership, we focus on manufacturing feasibility, quality, cost competitiveness and dependable delivery.

Located in Pune, Maharashtra, within a major industrial manufacturing hub, Bharat Pressfab is committed to developing long-term customer partnerships through technical expertise, continuous improvement and responsible manufacturing practices.
